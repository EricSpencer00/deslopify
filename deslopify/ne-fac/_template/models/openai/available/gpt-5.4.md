## Avoid unrelated creature metaphors (`openai-no-unrelated-creatures`)

**Writing · Don’t · Model: openai / gpt-5.4 (available)**

**When:** Any writing — GPT-5.4 Thinking · historical Nerdy personality

Do not introduce goblins or other creature metaphors into unrelated prose.

**Example — follows the rule:**

The build failed because a dependency is missing.

**Counterexample — breaks the rule:**

A dependency goblin sabotaged the build.

**Why:** The metaphor distracts from the actual cause.

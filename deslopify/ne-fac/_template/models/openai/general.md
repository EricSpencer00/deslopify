## Use creature metaphors only when they fit (`openai-no-unfitting-creatures`)

**Writing · Don’t · Company: openai**

**When:** Any writing

Do not add creature metaphors unless they fit the author’s meaning. Ex. Goblins

**Example — follows the rule:**

Keep the goblin in a fantasy scene that already features goblins.

**Counterexample — breaks the rule:**

Add “the latency goblin” to a plain performance report.

**Why:** A metaphor should fit the writer’s meaning and context.

## Keep distinct passages distinct (`minimax-preserve-distinct-passages`)

**Writing · Don’t · Model: minimax / minimax-m3 (available)**

**When:** Fiction — MiniMax M3

Do not flatten distinct passages into the same dry phrasing.

**Example — follows the rule:**

Keep one narrator’s spare tone and another’s exuberant cadence.

**Counterexample — breaks the rule:**

Rewrite both narrators into identical short, dry statements.

**Why:** Editing should not erase the differences between voices.

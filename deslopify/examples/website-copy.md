# Website copy: say what the product does

Before:

> Meet LedgerBox, a powerful solution designed to empower your team. LedgerBox exports CSV reports for up to 20 projects. The beta may omit archived projects. Read https://example.org/limits before importing.

After:

> LedgerBox exports CSV reports for up to 20 projects. The beta may omit archived projects. Read https://example.org/limits before importing.

The concrete capability replaces unsupported praise. Keep the limit, beta qualification, product name, technical format, and link. Preserve accessible labels and required disclosures when editing a real page.

## FamousMoji: keep the artwork choice, remove internal accounting

Before:

> Swap album / Get That Yam Off Your Face / 2,554 Printify items · 53,634 / 53,634 rendered

After, when album/artwork selection works:

> Choose an album / Get That Yam Off Your Face

The provider/catalog count and render accounting do not help a shopper choose or use the product; remove them from public copy or move them to internal diagnostics. Keep the artwork title and a usable choice in ordinary language. Omit an unusable control; do not imply it works. Useful customer-facing specs such as the CSV format and 20-project limit above stay exact.

## Let the product card do its work

For a card whose photo, product heading, price, and working purchase control already communicate the offer:

Before:

> Explore Our Stunning Collection / Artwork Preview / Get That Yam Off Your Face — 12 × 18 in matte poster. / Choose a size before adding to cart. / Add to cart

After:

> Get That Yam Off Your Face — 12 × 18 in matte poster. / Choose a size before adding to cart. / Add to cart

The decorative introduction and preview label repeat the content and layout. The product heading, size/material facts, instruction, and purchase label still help the shopper. Preserve accessible names and alt text, conditional stock errors, and required returns/legal disclosures in their existing roles. Keep a heading when it provides context or navigation; a photo does not replace every reader's access to that information.

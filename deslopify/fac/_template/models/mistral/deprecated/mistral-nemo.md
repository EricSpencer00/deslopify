## Make alternatives meaningfully different (`mistral-distinct-alternatives`)

**Writing · Do · Model: mistral / mistral-nemo (deprecated)**

**When:** Fiction — Mistral Nemo · Q8 · KoboldCPP

Make requested alternatives meaningfully different.

**Example — follows the rule:**

Offer a quiet reconciliation and a tense unresolved encounter as two endings.

**Counterexample — breaks the rule:**

Offer the same ending three times with different adjectives.

**Why:** Different options should give the author a real choice.

## Paper writing

- Delete repetition that adds no information. Nothing in a single slide should be filler for things that have already been said, especially titles and visual metadata.
- Use a supported number or example when it makes a vague claim clearer.
- Let the argument determine paragraph length and structure. Do not arbitrarily limit or extend length of text to fit a predefined amount.
- Keep the author’s voice while making the requested edits.

## Any writing

- Choose wording for meaning, not for a stock phrase checklist.
